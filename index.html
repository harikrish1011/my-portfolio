About

Hari focuses on creating precise, well-functioning systems — the kind where a poultry farmer or a grape grower stops touching the pesticide directly. Most of his projects follow the same instinct: find the step in a process that causes injury, mess, or repetition, then automate it with basic, reliable hardware.

He enjoys the collaborative side of engineering as much as the build itself, and is eager to join a team where that curiosity and attention to detail can be put to use on real problems.
